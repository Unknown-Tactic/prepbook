Get your study out of Lichess
  1. Open your study on lichess.org in Safari.
  2. Under the board, tap the share tab (the arrow/share icon), then tap Study PGN to download every chapter at once. On iPhone it saves to the Files app, in Downloads.
  3. Come back here, tap Choose PGN file, and pick that file. Or copy the PGN text and paste it in the box.
  4. Tap Import. Each file becomes its own study here. Lichess remembers whether each chapter is for White or Black, and we use that.

Your studies

Each study keeps its own progress. Switch a chapter off to leave it out of training without deleting it. Updated a study on Lichess? Use “Replace with updated PGN” on that study and it starts fresh.

Game check

Compares your real games as White with your studies and shows exactly where you, or your opponent, left your prep. Switch colour at the top to check your games with the other colour.

How to check your games
  1. Type your username below. A download link appears.
  2. Tap the link. Your games download as a file to the Files app, in Downloads.
  3. Come back, tap Choose games file and pick it. You can pick a Lichess file and a Chess.com file together.
  4. Tap Compare with my prep.
